THREE TOOLS, THREE PURPOSES
"AI," "Copilot," "Charlie," and "ChatGPT" get used interchangeably around the office. They are not interchangeable in practice — each one lives in a different place, and each one comes with different rules for what you can put into it.

Here's the quick version before we go hands-on together in September.
WHICH ONE SHOULD I USE?
Three questions, and the third one is the one to remember:
  1. Have a CFS policy, process, or “how do I…” question? Ask Charlie. It's our own assistant, built for CFS and grounded in our actual policies, and it's available to every staff member on the intranet — no license required.
  2. Drafting or summarizing inside Word, Outlook, or Teams? Use Microsoft Copilot if your account is licensed for it. It lives inside the Microsoft 365 apps you already use and stays within CFS's Microsoft environment.
  3. Thinking about using ChatGPT, Gemini, or another public AI tool for work? Hold off. These tools sit outside CFS's environment and are not covered under our data protections — they are not approved for agency work at this time.
Knowing which tool is which now means our September training can focus on how to use them well, instead of sorting out what's what.
Pro tip: Not sure which tool you're looking at? Check where it lives. Charlie is on the intranet. Copilot lives inside your Microsoft 365 apps. If it's neither of those, it's not a CFS-managed tool.
