NEW SHARP PRINTERS ARE HEADING TO THE HOUSES
Starting this week, Central Business Systems is delivering new Sharp printers to our group homes, beginning with sites in Brooklyn, Jamaica, the Bronx, Yonkers, and Manhattan. Each new Sharp replaces the Xerox at that house. Not every home is on this week's schedule, so if nobody has contacted your residence yet, keep printing exactly as you do today.

Before your Sharp arrives, you'll receive an email directly from PaperCut with your personal six digit security PIN. Save that email somewhere you can find it. The PIN is what releases your print jobs at the new device, and it's the same PIN whether you're at your house, the office, or any other CFS site with a Sharp. Personal desktop printers are not part of this change and keep working as always.
HOW PRINTING WORKS ONCE THE SHARP IS IN
Three steps, and the last one is the only new habit:
  1. Print to “CFS Papercut” (it may show as “CFS Papercut on CFSAZ-PRINT01”). Pick that queue instead of a specific printer. Your job doesn't print yet; it waits for you.
  2. Walk to the Sharp and enter your six digit PIN on the panel. Your held jobs appear; select what you want and it prints while you stand there.
  3. Pick it up within 10 hours. Jobs that aren't released in that window are deleted automatically, so if you forget, just print it again.
No PIN email? Check your junk folder first, then email HelpDesk@cfsny.org and we'll send it again. Panel asking for a username and password instead of a PIN? Don't guess; let HelpDesk know which house and we'll fix it on our end. Need to scan? Scan to email works the same way it did on the Xerox.
Pro tip: That PIN is yours alone, so please don't share it or tape it to the printer. The whole point of hold and release is that documents about the people we support only come out when the person who printed them is standing at the device. It's a small step that keeps PHI off the output tray.
