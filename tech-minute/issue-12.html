Good morning CFS Family,

A big milestone this week: all five main offices (NY, Bronx, Long Island, Staten Island, and Hackensack NJ) now have their new Sharp printers in place, replacing our old Xerox and Canon devices. Printing on them works a little differently, and it all comes down to one thing: your security PIN.
CFS TECH MINUTE
Issue #12 | August 25, 2026
YOUR PIN RELEASES YOUR PRINT JOBS
On the new Sharp devices, print jobs no longer come out the moment you click Print. When you print to the CFS Papercut queue, your job is held securely until you walk up to any new Sharp printer, enter your six digit security PIN on the screen, and release it yourself. Your pages never sit in the tray for someone else to pick up, and you can release your job at any new Sharp printer at any CFS office, not just the one down the hall.

Your PIN arrived by email. Before your office's printers were replaced, PaperCut sent every staff member an automatic email containing their personal PIN. If you have not saved it yet, search your inbox for PaperCut and keep that email somewhere safe. (Personal desktop printers are not part of this change and work exactly as before.)
HOW TO PRINT ON THE NEW SHARPS
Three steps, start to finish:
  1. Print as usual and choose the CFS Papercut queue in your print menu. It may appear as “CFS Papercut on CFSAZ-PRINT01,” and that is the same queue. Your job is held securely instead of printing right away.
  2. Walk to any new Sharp printer, enter your six digit PIN on the screen, and select your job to release it. Any Sharp printer at any CFS office will work.
  3. Never got your PIN email? Check your inbox for a message from PaperCut first. If it is not there, reach out to the HelpDesk at HelpDesk@cfsny.org and we will get you printing.
One PIN, any Sharp printer, at any CFS office. That is the whole system.
Pro tip: A held job waits for you for up to 10 hours. If you do not release it within that window, it is deleted automatically. So if your job is not on the screen when you arrive at the printer, simply print it again.
