SAY IT THE WAY YOU'D SAY IT
Not everyone does their best thinking at a keyboard. Maybe typing is slow for you. Maybe English is your second or third language. Or maybe you know exactly what you need, but it takes three tries to get it typed out, and by then you've left out half the details.

With voice, you just tell Copilot what you need, the same way you would explain it to a coworker. And you can say it in your own language. Copilot understands about 48 languages, including Spanish, French, Chinese, Russian, Arabic, Korean, and Portuguese, and it answers you back in the same language you spoke. That means you can describe the whole situation, with every detail that matters, instead of settling for whatever you managed to type.
TWO BUTTONS, TWO WAYS TO TALK
Open the Microsoft 365 Copilot app on your computer or phone and look at the right side of the message box:
  1. The microphone: speak instead of type. Copilot turns your words into text in the message box. Read it over, fix anything it misheard, then press send. This is perfect for longer requests.
  2. The sound wave: have a conversation. Copilot listens and answers you out loud, and you can go back and forth just like a phone call. When you finish, the whole conversation is saved as text in your chat history.
  3. “Hey Copilot” for hands free. In the Windows app, turn on “Listen for Hey Copilot” in Settings. The wake word itself is in English, but once the chat starts, speak whatever language you like.
Don't see the sound wave? It isn't available on your account yet. Copilot inside Teams can take dictation, but for a spoken conversation, use the Microsoft 365 Copilot app. And voice or typed, the rule from last week still applies: anything headed into a record or a note to a family gets checked before it's saved or sent.
Pro tip: Before you talk to Copilot about a person you support, look around. If you wouldn't say it out loud in the hallway, type it instead. Voice is great for thinking out loud and drafting, but anyone nearby can hear every word, so save names and personal details for typing.
Try this with Copilot: Click the sound wave and say, in your own language, “What can you help me with today?” Then ask it to help with one real task on your list.
