YOUR DAY, SUMMARIZED BEFORE YOU OPEN A SINGLE EMAIL
If you have a Microsoft 365 Copilot license, Outlook can start your morning with a short brief: what came in overnight, which messages look like they need a reply from you, and what's on your calendar today. Copilot reads your own mailbox and calendar (nothing anyone else can see) and pulls the important pieces to the top.

Think of it as the ten minutes of inbox triage you already do every morning, finished before your coffee is poured. You still read the emails that matter. You just don't have to hunt for them first.
HOW TO TRY IT
Three steps, about thirty seconds:
  1. Open Outlook and select the Copilot icon in the ribbon at the top of the window.
  2. Ask in plain English: "Give me a brief of my day. What needs my attention in email, and what's on my calendar?"
  3. Read it, then decide. Click into anything Copilot flags. It summarizes; you still call the shots (and, as we covered two weeks ago, it can be wrong, so check before you act).
