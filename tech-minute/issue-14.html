YOUR DAY, SUMMARIZED BEFORE YOU OPEN A SINGLE EMAIL
Open the new Outlook and look at the very top of your inbox, above your newest message. That card labeled Your morning briefing is Copilot's summary of your day: what came in overnight, which messages look like they need a reply from you, and what's on your calendar today. Copilot reads your own mailbox and calendar (nothing anyone else can see) and pulls the important pieces to the top. You don't need a Copilot license to see it. It's there for everyone.

Think of it as the ten minutes of inbox triage you already do every morning, finished before your coffee is poured. You still read the emails that matter. You just don't have to hunt for them first.
HOW TO USE IT
Three steps, about thirty seconds:
  1. Open the new Outlook (desktop or web) and find the Your morning briefing card at the top of your inbox. If it's collapsed, click it to expand.
  2. Ask a follow up right from the card: "What needs a decision from me today?" or "Draft a reply to the most urgent one." It's a conversation, not just a summary.
  3. Read it, then decide. Click into anything Copilot flags. It summarizes; you still call the shots, so check the original before you act.
Don't see the card? You may still be on classic Outlook. Look for the Try the new Outlook toggle in the top right, or check Outlook on the web at outlook.office.com.
