CFS TECH MINUTE
Issue #9 | July 28, 2026
SET YOURSELF UP TODAY (ONE MINUTE)
This works for your CFS agency account, the email address ending in @cfsny.org and its password. It does not reset passwords for other systems like Therap or your personal accounts.

Before you ever need a reset, take one minute to confirm your verification methods at aka.ms/ssprsetup. Most of you already have the Authenticator app from our MFA rollout; just confirm a second method (text or email) is listed. Got a new phone recently? Re-add your Authenticator while you are there; it takes about a minute and saves you a headache later.
LOCKED OUT? TAKE THE FAST LANE
Skip the ticket, skip the wait. The fastest way to get back into your account is to reset it yourself:
  1. Go to aka.ms/sspr from any browser, including your personal phone.
  2. Enter your CFS email address and follow the two verification prompts.
  3. Choose a new password that meets our password policy.
  4. Sign in with your new password. That is it, you are back in.
The helpdesk is always here if you get stuck, but most of you will be back in before we could even pick up the phone.

One password, two doors: your Paycom sign-in uses your CFS email address and that same password. Reset one, and you have fixed both, so payday never catches you locked out.
Pro tip: Do the one-minute setup at aka.ms/ssprsetup today, while you still know your password, and bookmark aka.ms/sspr on your phone. The fast lane only helps if it is open before you need it.
